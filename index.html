Chat GPT Ads (2026): Who Sees Them and Where They Run

Chat GPT ads are live, but not for everyone: only logged-in adults on two subscription tiers ever see one. Here is where the pilot runs today and what a buyer gets.

Who actually sees chat gpt ads

This is the fact that decides whether the channel is relevant to you, and most coverage buries it. The test runs for logged-in adult users on the Free and Go subscription tiers only. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education tiers carry no advertising at all. So the reachable audience is the free and entry-paid population, not the professional and corporate users who tend to appear in a B2B media plan. If you sell developer tooling, enterprise software or anything bought by someone whose employer pays for a Business seat, your buyer is structurally excluded from this inventory. If you sell to consumers comparing products, the exclusion barely matters. Work out which of those you are before reading another word about targeting, because no amount of creative fixes an audience that is not there.

The rollout, in order

The timeline is public and worth reading as a trajectory rather than a status. Testing began in the United States on 9 February 2026. On 26 March the company reported early results it characterised as encouraging, citing no measurable impact on consumer trust, low dismissal rates and improving relevance, and announced expansion beyond the United States beginning with Canada, Australia and New Zealand. On 7 May it named the next wave: the United Kingdom, Mexico, Brazil, Japan and South Korea. On 11 August it confirmed those five had launched, with more markets promised during the year. Each step took roughly two months. That cadence is the useful signal for planning, because it suggests the inventory you evaluate this quarter will be meaningfully larger by the next one.

The rules the company set itself

Four commitments are stated repeatedly and they shape what the format can be. Answers stay independent: advertising does not influence what the assistant tells someone. Conversations stay private from advertisers, which rules out the kind of raw intent data buyers might assume they are getting. Ads carry clear labels in the interface and remain visually distinct from the response itself. And users are given control over their experience. The stated purpose is supporting broader free access rather than maximising revenue per session. For an advertiser, the practical reading is this: you are buying placement next to a moment of consideration, not a seat inside the recommendation. Anyone selling you influence over what the assistant recommends is describing something other than this product.

What a buyer gets today

Signup runs through the advertising site, which also offers a sales support request for larger accounts. New advertisers are promoted 500 dollars in ad credit after spending 500 dollars, terms worth confirming before you build a plan on it. After signup you land in Ads Manager, where the documented flow is three steps: set up the campaign with a budget and a goal, add ad details either individually or by bulk upload, then launch and measure. Reporting covers impressions, clicks and spend. Named early advertisers include Newegg, Best Buy, Lowe's and VistaPrint, and a Newegg performance marketing manager is quoted describing the goal as being part of the conversation while somebody researches a purchase. That framing is the most accurate description of the format anyone has offered.

Testing the channel properly

  1. Check your audience is eligible
    If your buyers sit on Plus, Pro, Business or Enterprise seats, they cannot be reached here at all. Settle that question before commissioning a single piece of creative.
  2. Confirm your market is live
    The rollout is country by country and still expanding. Verify current availability where you sell rather than assuming a launch announcement covered your region.
  3. Write for the comparison moment
    The format sits beside somebody weighing options, not searching a keyword. Creative that answers a tradeoff will outperform a slogan, because the surrounding context is a decision.
  4. Fund it as an experiment
    The vendor still calls this a test that keeps evolving. Size the budget so a format change mid-quarter is an inconvenience rather than a hole in the annual plan.
